# Student flows

1. Open **Students** to search and page through student records.
2. Use **New student** to enter student and fee details. If the initial payment is nonzero, student and payment creation use the atomic creation flow.
3. Open a student to review their profile, payments, invoice links, and certificate route.

CSV import with dry-run and row-level errors, export, edit, soft-delete, and duplicate-phone warnings are not connected yet. Do not treat the list's create/details actions as completion of those workflows.
