# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct their impact.
- Focus feedback on the work and its effect, not on personal traits.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's configured security contact. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate corrective action. Do not use public issue threads to disclose private or sensitive allegations.

This policy applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
